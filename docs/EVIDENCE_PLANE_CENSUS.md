# Six-vendor first-party evidence-plane census

COPS-000093, Todd; observed/researched 2026-10-03. Common base `613c3a13c0e52088eeb33d6266b6ca7b2b783000`. Research and this authority decision precede implementation. Astra's FriendlyELEC Foundation 7A is a separate tranche; none of its unfinished files, rows, branch or Mission are evidence here.

## Method and limits

Parent-supplied parallel primary-source researchers inspected the six vendors' public surfaces, ownership chains, representative documents and counterexamples. Todd independently inspected the exact common-base collectors/contracts and captured three Radxa pages' actual bytes/headers at 07:16 UTC. The POC's three-pass qualification measures hashes; the other vendors' volatility ratings below are structural risk assessments, not measured stability. Failed public-research retrieval is not proof of vendor outage, withdrawal or EOL. Indexed Orange Pi pages do not establish current native freshness. Nothing third-party, including CNX, enters the implementation.

Each row below inherits its vendor's explicitly stated transport/index/retention/failure attributes. Row overrides are stated where observed. `UNKNOWN` means not established, including absence of a feed/API unless actually tested. Version, notification, effect, upload, page modification, software build and observation dates are separate facts. An old page found today is historical backfill.

Plane names are research roles, not new taxonomy requirements: LIFECYCLE is a claim role; REFERENCE is historical/supporting material; CERTIFICATION corresponds to existing REGULATORY semantics. Hosting an official community page is proof of the publisher, not of a manufacturer-authored commitment. Identity always belongs to Board Clank's existing model. Category/scope is checked before linkage.

## Priorities, rejected candidates and POC decision

1. **Radxa ROCK 5B Hardware Design DOCUMENTATION references**: structured model tab, explicit hardware tokens/component notes, stable heading anchors, official model-bearing artifact paths and one already-owned Board. Rejected for this tranche: duplicate PIP/old wiki/mirror observations; broad news or forum ingestion; community software releases; whole-shop hashes; stock-to-EOL inference; certificate-to-variant/launch inference; opaque numbered-URL identity; nonboard devices/accessories/carriers/assembled bundles; unverified feeds/APIs; arbitrary external downloads; undated seller 'new version' promoted as model-wide revision. Rejection means unsuitable for this bounded POC, not that the retained first-party evidence is useless.

## Cross-plane identity and architecture gate

No fuzzy merging. A reference may be `REFERENCE_TO_BOARD` only after exact vendor/product identity and explicit first-party model-bearing context match an existing PRODUCT Board. Missing or conflicting signals fail closed, with unresolved evidence retained. It cannot create another vendor's entity or a free-standing Board. Historical backfill silently baselines even after the source's first pass; timestamps are never invented; old announcements/revision documents do not become current NEW_BOARD/FIELD_CHANGED.

The actual common-base model failure, local alternatives, namespace contract, migration/observer effects and qualification gates are in [EVIDENCE_PLANE_ARCHITECTURE.md](EVIDENCE_PLANE_ARCHITECTURE.md). The POC adds one `EntityKind.DOCUMENTATION_REFERENCE` member: a generic model contract addition, with no SQL migration, generic pipeline change or PRODUCT adapter change. Its first-party manual live path uses an isolated DB/worktree, all sources disabled, freeze preserved, scheduler NONE, delivery NONE. No production/NAS deployment or integration with Astra is authorized here.
