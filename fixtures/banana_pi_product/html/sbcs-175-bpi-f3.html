BPI-F3

Overview

SpacemiT K1 8 core RISC-V chip AI 2.0Tops from RISC-V Core. Computing power equivalent to 1.3 times Cortex-A55.

Hardware

Memory: 2GB/4GB/8GB/16GB LPDDR4 (Supports up to 16G LPDDR4). Storage: 2/4/8/16G eMMC flash, 4M SPI NOR.

Wireless

2.4G/5G WiFi and Bluetooth 4.2.

Ethernet

2x GbE Ethernet port (supports PoE with add-on PoE HAT).
