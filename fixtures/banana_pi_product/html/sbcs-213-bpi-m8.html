BPI-M8

Overview

BPI-M8 features the Allwinner A733 octa-core processor with Arm Mali-G57 GPU (ArcherVR BXM-4-64 MC1), a 3TOPS NPU, and LPDDR4, LPDDR4x, LPDDR5 DRAM and UFS/eMMC memory.

Hardware

Memory: 4GB LPDDR5. Storage: 32GB eMMC.

Wireless

Onboard KEIIOT K038 WiFi/Bluetooth chip. Two 12x12mm single-antenna modules.

USB

USB, DP, UFS, HDMI, MIPI CSI, MIPI DSI, FAN, RTC interfaces.
