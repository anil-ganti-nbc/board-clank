BPI-M2 Zero

Overview

CPU: Allwinner H2+/H3, Quad-core Cortex-A7 512MB DDR3 SDRAM WiFi & BT onboard Mini HDMI 40 Pin Raspberry Pi-compatible GPIO.

Hardware

Memory (SDRAM) 512M DDR3(shared with GPU) Onboard Storage TF card (Max. 64GB) Onboard flash 8G eMMC (variant dependent).

Comparison

H3(option H2+/H5): the board shipped in multiple SoC options across production runs.
