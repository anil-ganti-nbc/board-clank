Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W

At its heart is a Raspberry Pi RP3A0 system-in-package (SiP), integrating a Broadcom BCM2710A1 die with 512MB of LPDDR2 SDRAM.

Overview

Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W is a form factor-compatible drop-in replacement for Raspberry Pi Zero W.

Processor: Broadcom BCM2710A1, quad-core 64-bit SoC (Arm Cortex-A53 @ 1GHz)

Memory: 512MB LPDDR2

Connectivity: 2.4GHz IEEE 802.11b/g/n wireless LAN, Bluetooth 4.2, BLE
