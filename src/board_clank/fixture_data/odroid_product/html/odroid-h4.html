Do It Yourself

The ODROID H-series offers you a lot of freedom. You are free to choose: 1. The amount and brand of memory. No soldered memory. 2. The size of the eMMC (including not using one). No soldered eMMC. 3. The size of the NVMe PCIe Gen 3 x4 SSD.

Supply note
