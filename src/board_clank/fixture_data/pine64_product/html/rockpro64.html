ROCKPro64

The ROCKPro64 is the most powerful single board computer on offer from PINE64, featuring a Rockchip RK3399 hexa-core SoC as well as a quad-core Mali T860 MP4 GPU and up-to 4GB of dual-channel LPDDR4 system memory. The board comes packed with features, including a USB 3.0 and USB type C with DP1.2 port, a full PCIe x4 as well as eMMC module socket.

Features and Specifications

  • SoC: 4 x ARM Cortex A53 cores @ 1.4GHz and 2 x ARM Cortex A72 cores @ 1.8 GHz (Rockchip RK3399)
  • Memory: LPDDR4 RAM (up to 4GB)
  • Networking: Gigabit Ethernet
