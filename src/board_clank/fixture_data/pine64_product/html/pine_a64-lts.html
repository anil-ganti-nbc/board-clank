PINE A64-LTS

The PINE A64-LTS is the long-term supply variant of the PINE A64 family, powered by the Allwinner R18 (industrial grade A64). The board ships without onboard Wi-Fi; connectivity is provided via the EXP module.

Features and Specifications

  • SoC: Allwinner R18 quad-core ARM Cortex A53
  • Memory: DDR3 RAM (up to 2GB)
  • Networking: Gigabit Ethernet via EXP module
