Ox64

The Ox64 Single Board Computer features a Bouffalo Lab BL808 RISC-V SoC with single 64-bit 480MHz RV64 C906 core and two 32-bit 320MHz RV32 E907 + 150MHz E902 cores, 728KB internal SRAM and 64MB internal PSRAM. It is equipped with 128MB NOR flash.

Features and Specifications

  • SoC: Bouffalo Lab BL808 RISC-V (RV64 C906 + dual RV32)
  • Memory: 64MB internal PSRAM
  • Storage: 128MB NOR flash, microSD slot
